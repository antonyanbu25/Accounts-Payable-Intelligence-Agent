# TDS Circular — Fees for Professional or Technical Services

**Circular No.:** CBDT-TDS/194J-2020
**Date of Issue:** 1 April 2020
**Effective From:** 1 April 2020 (in force, no sunset date)
**Subject:** Tax deduction at source under Section 194J of the Income Tax
Act, 1961, on payments for professional or technical services, including
information technology and management consulting engagements.

## Rule

Any payer making a payment to a resident for fees for professional or
technical services — including services classifiable under SAC 998313
(information technology design and development) and SAC 998311
(management and business consulting) — shall deduct tax at source under
Section 194J of the Income Tax Act, 1961, at the rate of **10%** of the
amount paid or credited, whichever is earlier.

This obligation applies irrespective of the Goods and Services Tax rate or
treatment applicable to the same transaction under the relevant GST rate
notification, which is governed separately.

## Basis of Computation

See the separate circular on the treatment of the Goods and Services Tax
component for the correct base amount on which this rate is to be applied.
