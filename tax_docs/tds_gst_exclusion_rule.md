# TDS Circular — Treatment of GST Component for the Purpose of Tax Deduction at Source

**Circular No.:** 23/2017 (Central Board of Direct Taxes)
**Date of Issue:** 19 July 2017
**Effective From:** 19 July 2017 (in force, no sunset date; applies to all
new and existing contracts)
**Subject:** Whether tax is to be deducted at source under Chapter XVII-B
of the Income Tax Act, 1961, on the Goods and Services Tax component of an
amount payable to a resident.

## Rule

Wherever the terms of an agreement or contract between the payer and the
payee stipulate that the Goods and Services Tax component of the amount
payable is indicated separately, tax shall be deducted at source under
Chapter XVII-B of the Income Tax Act, 1961, on the amount paid or payable
**without including** such Goods and Services Tax component.

Goods and Services Tax, for this purpose, includes Central Goods and
Services Tax, State Goods and Services Tax, Integrated Goods and Services
Tax, and Union Territory Goods and Services Tax.

## Rationale

The Goods and Services Tax collected by the payee on the transaction is
not, in substance, income in the hands of the payee — it is a statutory
levy collected on behalf of the Government and subsequently remitted. Tax
deduction at source under Chapter XVII-B is a mechanism for collecting tax
in advance on the payee's income, and should not be applied to an amount
that does not constitute the payee's income.

## Applicability

This rule applies to all sections under which tax is deductible at source
on payments to residents, including but not limited to Sections 194C and
194J, wherever the invoice or contract states the base value and the
applicable GST component as separate, identifiable line items — which is
the standard practice reflected in every vendor invoice recorded in this
system.
