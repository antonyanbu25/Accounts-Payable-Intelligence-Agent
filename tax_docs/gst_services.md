# Rate Notification — Professional and Business Consulting Services

**Notification No.:** CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate) 11/2017, as continued in force
**Date of Issue:** 28 June 2017
**Effective From:** 1 July 2017 (no change made by the September 2025 rate
rationalization exercise)
**Subject:** Applicable Goods and Services Tax rate on management,
business, and professional consulting services, classified under SAC
998311.

## Rate

Services falling under SAC **998311** (management consulting and general
business advisory services, including strategic, financial, HR, and
operational consulting engagements) attract Goods and Services Tax at the
aggregate rate of **18%**.

This rate remained unchanged following the rate rationalization exercise
that took effect 22 September 2025.

## Applicability

This notification applies uniformly across all States and Union
Territories; the CGST+SGST vs. IGST split is governed separately by the
circular on Place of Supply. This SAC covers general consulting
engagements and is distinct from information technology design and
development services (SAC 998313), which are addressed in a separate
notification.
