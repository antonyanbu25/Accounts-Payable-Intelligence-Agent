# Rate Notification — Outdoor Catering and Corporate Food Services

**Notification No.:** CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate) 46/2017
**Date of Issue:** 14 November 2017
**Effective From:** 15 November 2017
**Effective To:** 21 September 2025 (superseded, see the amending
notification referenced in the corresponding filing for the following
period)
**Subject:** Applicable Goods and Services Tax rate on outdoor catering
and corporate food services, classified under SAC 996331.

## Rate

Services falling under SAC **996331** (outdoor catering, corporate
cafeteria management, and event food services) attract Goods and Services
Tax at the aggregate rate of **18%**.

## Applicability

This notification applied uniformly across all States and Union
Territories for the period stated above.
