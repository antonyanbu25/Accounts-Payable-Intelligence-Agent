# Rate Notification — Furniture

**Notification No.:** CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate), as continued in force
**Date of Issue:** 28 June 2017
**Effective From:** 1 July 2017 (no change made by the September 2025 rate
rationalization exercise)
**Subject:** Applicable Goods and Services Tax rate on furniture and seating,
classified under HSN Chapter 94, sub-heading 9403.

## Rate

Goods falling under HSN code **9403** (furniture and parts thereof,
including office furniture, seating, tables, and storage units, whether of
wood, metal, or other material) attract Goods and Services Tax at the
aggregate rate of **18%**.

This rate remained unchanged following the rate rationalization exercise
that took effect 22 September 2025, and continues to apply without a
sunset or review date.

## Applicability

This notification applies uniformly across all States and Union
Territories. The manner in which the 18% aggregate rate is levied — as
CGST + SGST or as IGST — is governed separately by the circular on Place of
Supply, and does not vary the rate itself.
