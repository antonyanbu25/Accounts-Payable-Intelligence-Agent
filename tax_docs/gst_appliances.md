# Rate Notification — Electrical Appliances

**Notification No.:** CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate), as continued in force
**Date of Issue:** 28 June 2017
**Effective From:** 1 July 2017 (no change made by the September 2025 rate
rationalization exercise)
**Subject:** Applicable Goods and Services Tax rate on electrical
appliances and equipment, classified under HSN Chapter 85, sub-heading 8516.

## Rate

Goods falling under HSN code **8516** (electric instantaneous or storage
water heaters, electric space-heating apparatus, hair dryers, and other
electro-thermic appliances of a kind used for domestic or office purposes)
attract Goods and Services Tax at the aggregate rate of **18%**.

This rate remained unchanged following the rate rationalization exercise
that took effect 22 September 2025, and continues to apply without a
sunset or review date.

## Applicability

This notification applies uniformly across all States and Union
Territories. The manner in which the 18% aggregate rate is levied — as
CGST + SGST or as IGST — is governed separately by the circular on Place of
Supply, and does not vary the rate itself.

Note: this classification is distinct from furniture and seating (HSN
9403), which is addressed under a separate notification. Office equipment
that also functions as furniture (e.g., a heated seating unit) should be
classified by its primary function.
