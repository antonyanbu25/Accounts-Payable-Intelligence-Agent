# Rate Notification — Information Technology Design and Development Services (Revised)

**Notification No.:** CBIC-RATE/2025-Central Tax (Rate) 07/2025
**Date of Issue:** 3 September 2025
**Effective From:** 22 September 2025
**Effective To:** (in force, no sunset date)
**Subject:** Revision of the Goods and Services Tax rate applicable to
information technology design, development, and consulting services,
classified under SAC 998313, pursuant to the rate rationalization exercise
of the GST Council.

## Rate

With effect from 22 September 2025, services falling under SAC **998313**
attract Goods and Services Tax at the revised aggregate rate of **12%**,
in place of the 18% rate previously notified under
CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate) 11/2017.

## Applicability

This revised rate applies uniformly across all States and Union
Territories, to all invoices dated on or after 22 September 2025. Invoices
dated before this date remain governed by the previously notified 18%
rate, irrespective of the date on which this notification is issued or
the date on which payment is made.
