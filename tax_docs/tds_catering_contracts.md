# TDS Circular — Payments to Contractors

**Circular No.:** CBDT-TDS/194C-2020
**Date of Issue:** 1 April 2020
**Effective From:** 1 April 2020 (in force, no sunset date)
**Subject:** Tax deduction at source under Section 194C of the Income Tax
Act, 1961, on payments to resident contractors, including outdoor
catering and corporate food service contracts.

## Rule

Any payer making a payment to a resident contractor for work carried out
under a contract — including outdoor catering, corporate cafeteria
management, and event food services classifiable under SAC 996331 — shall
deduct tax at source under Section 194C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, at
the rate of **2%** of the amount paid or credited to a contractor that is
not an individual or Hindu Undivided Family, whichever is earlier.

This obligation applies irrespective of the Goods and Services Tax rate or
treatment applicable to the same transaction under the relevant GST rate
notification, which is governed separately.

## Basis of Computation

See the separate circular on the treatment of the Goods and Services Tax
component for the correct base amount on which this rate is to be applied.
Furniture, appliance, and other pure goods purchases are not payments
under a works contract and do not fall within the scope of this circular.
