# Rate Notification — Construction Materials

**Notification No.:** CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate) 21/2017
**Date of Issue:** 28 June 2017
**Effective From:** 1 July 2017
**Subject:** Applicable Goods and Services Tax rate on cement, aggregates,
and other bulk construction materials, classified under HSN Chapter 68 and
Chapter 25.

## Rate

Goods falling under HSN codes **6810** (articles of cement, concrete, or
artificial stone) and **2523** (Portland cement and related hydraulic
cements) attract Goods and Services Tax at the aggregate rate of **28%**.

## Applicability

This notification applies to bulk construction and building-materials
purchases and is unrelated to office furniture (HSN 9403) or any other
category of purchase made by this organization.
