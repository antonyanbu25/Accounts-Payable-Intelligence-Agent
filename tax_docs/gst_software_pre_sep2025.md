# Rate Notification — Information Technology Design and Development Services

**Notification No.:** CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate) 11/2017
**Date of Issue:** 28 June 2017
**Effective From:** 1 July 2017
**Effective To:** 21 September 2025 (superseded — see Notification
CBIC-RATE/2025-Central Tax (Rate) 07/2025)
**Subject:** Applicable Goods and Services Tax rate on information
technology design, development, and consulting services, classified under
SAC 998313.

## Rate

Services falling under SAC **998313** (design, development, programming,
customization, and consulting services relating to software and
information systems) attract Goods and Services Tax at the aggregate rate
of **18%**.

## Applicability

This notification applied uniformly across all States and Union
Territories for the period stated above. It has since been superseded in
respect of the rate only, by Notification CBIC-RATE/2025-Central Tax
(Rate) 07/2025, effective 22 September 2025. This notification is retained
in this corpus for reference in respect of invoices dated within its
period of effect.
