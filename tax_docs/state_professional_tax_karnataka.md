# Karnataka State Professional Tax — Employer Notification

**Notification No.:** KA-CTD/PT/2023-04
**Date of Issue:** 1 April 2023
**Effective From:** 1 April 2023
**Issuing Authority:** Karnataka Commercial Taxes Department
**Subject:** Professional tax payable by employers in respect of employees
based at establishments within the State of Karnataka.

## Rule

Every employer with an establishment registered within the State of
Karnataka is liable to deduct and remit Professional Tax from the salary
of each employee whose gross monthly salary exceeds the threshold
prescribed under the Karnataka Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and
Employments Act.

## Scope

This levy applies to **employee compensation** at establishments located
within Karnataka. It has no application to Goods and Services Tax on
vendor purchases, invoices, or payments to suppliers, and should not be
confused with the CGST/SGST/IGST treatment governed by the circular on
Place of Supply, which applies nationally and is unrelated to this State-
specific employer obligation.
