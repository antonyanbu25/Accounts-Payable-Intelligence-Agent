# Circular on Determination of Place of Supply for Goods and Services

**Notification No.:** CBIC-GEN/2019-Central Tax
**Date of Issue:** 1 July 2019
**Effective From:** 1 July 2019
**Subject:** Applicability of Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) and State
Goods and Services Tax (SGST) versus Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST)
based on the location of the supplier and the place of supply.

## Background

Under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) framework, every taxable supply of
goods or services attracts tax at a single aggregate rate, as prescribed for
the relevant HSN or SAC classification by the applicable rate notification.
This circular does not prescribe rates; it prescribes how that single
aggregate rate is to be split and levied.

## Rule

1. Where the location of the supplier and the place of supply of the goods
   or services are in the **same State**, the supply shall be treated as an
   intra-State supply, and the applicable tax shall be levied as **Central
   Goods and Services Tax (CGST)** and **State Goods and Services Tax
   (SGST)** in equal halves of the aggregate rate.

2. Where the location of the supplier and the place of supply are in
   **different States**, the supply shall be treated as an inter-State
   supply, and the applicable tax shall be levied in full as **Integrated
   Goods and Services Tax (IGST)**, at the same aggregate rate that would
   otherwise have applied as CGST plus SGST combined.

3. For the purposes of this circular, the "location of the supplier" is the
   State in which the supplier is registered, as reflected in the first two
   digits of its Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN). The
   "place of supply", for a supply of goods or services to a registered
   business recipient, is ordinarily the location of the recipient's
   registered business establishment to which the supply is made.

## Illustration

A vendor registered in Karnataka supplying goods to a recipient's office
also located in Karnataka shall charge CGST and SGST. The same vendor
supplying identical goods to a recipient's office located in Maharashtra
shall instead charge IGST, at the same combined percentage.

This determination has no bearing on the applicable rate itself, which is
governed separately by the category-specific rate notifications.
