# Amendment Notification No. 07/2025-Central Tax (Rate)

In exercise of the powers conferred by the Central Goods and Services Tax
Act, and in partial modification of Notification No. CBIC-RATE/2017-Central
Tax (Rate) 46/2017 dated 14 November 2017, the Central Government, on the
recommendation of the Council, hereby notifies that, with effect from the
twenty-second day of September, 2025, the rate specified in the said
notification in respect of outdoor catering and corporate food services
falling under SAC 996331 shall stand revised from eighteen per cent (18%)
to five per cent (5%).

All other provisions of Notification No. CBIC-RATE/2017-Central Tax (Rate)
46/2017, including its scope of applicability to outdoor catering,
corporate cafeteria management, and event food services, and its
application uniformly across all States and Union Territories, shall
continue to apply mutatis mutandis and are not otherwise disturbed by this
amendment.

This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in
the Official Gazette.
